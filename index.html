About

I design the path from sunlight to the socket: converters that track the panel, inverters that shape the current, and the controls that keep it running.

Renewable energy and electric mobility engineer, graduated from the Faculty of Sciences and Techniques of Marrakech. I like the moment a simulation stops being a picture and becomes a working board, a PLC program or a SCADA screen someone relies on.

Before the engineering cycle, a DEUST in mathematics, computer science and physics in Errachidia. Alongside it, evening maths classes for DEUST students and the media cell of the student club.
